### 6.5 The shipped GEMM's schedule, read from the ISA — **done** ✅ (2026-09-29)

**Question** (KERNELS.md H2, H3, G-o2, G-o3): the fp16 GEMM
(`dit_gemm_wg128x256_bt16_swz8`: WM 4 × WN 8, 2×2 waves, `BK_TILES 1`,
tiled B, band 8) sits at 74% of the ceiling at the clock it runs
(§0.6). Is the loop latency-bound, issue-bound, or memory-bound, and
does occupancy matter?

**The loop** (`cmd/probe`, `RADV_DEBUG=asm`; wave64, 240 VGPRs, no
spill, no LDS). One iteration is one 16-wide K tile: 56 SALU + 29 VALU
of address arithmetic **recomputed every iteration** (20 `s_delay_alu`
stalls inside it) → 24 `buffer_load_b128` in 3 clauses (4 A + 8 B
fragments) → `s_waitcnt vmcnt(14)` → 4 `v_wmma` → `vmcnt(12)` → 4 → …
→ `vmcnt(0)` → the last 4 → branch. 32 MMAs, ~175 instructions, 4.5
non-MMA per MMA. **ACO does not prefetch across iterations**: every K
tile pays one exposed latency, from the address math to the tenth load.

**Occupancy is not the lever.** `-DLDS_PAD=n` (an unused `shared`
array that caps workgroups a WGP, nothing else changed), run through
`H3_GEMM_SPV` in H3's `TestGPUShapes` at 480p, int8: 16 KB / 32 KB /
64 KB of pad → 4 / 2 / **1** workgroups a CU, and `gemm qkv` reads
36.9 / 37.2 / 37.3 / 37.6 TFLOP/s (shipped first), gate/up 36.6 → 37.0,
`o` 34.5 → 34.7, a forward 30.10 → 30.00 s. **Flat to 1% down to one
4-wave workgroup a CU.** (ACO reports "6 subgroups a SIMD" for this
build, which the 1536-register arithmetic at wave64 does not give and
which the flat result makes moot.)

**The memory system is ~10 points; the in-wave schedule ~16.**
`-DL0_LOADS=1` keeps every load and every wait but points them all at
two K tiles, so each is an L0 hit (the result is garbage by
construction; `TestGPUShapes` times a loaded build anyway):

| | real loads | L0 loads |
|---|---|---|
| gemm qkv / gate / up / o (TFLOP/s) | 36.9 / 36.6 / 36.6 / 34.5 | **43.6 / 44.2 / 44.0 / 42.8** |
| sclk during the forward (MHz) | 2600–2650 | 2650–2700 |
| per clock (FLOP/clk/CU, of 480) | **353, 74%** | **~405, 82–85%** |

(`gemm down` runs the KRANGE build, which the hook does not replace:
26.7 → 29.9 is the clock alone, a control.)

**What it says.** With the loads perfect the loop still stalls once a
tile — the address arithmetic, the issue of 24 loads and the L0 latency
to the tenth, in front of ~1,090 clocks of MMA — and at 1 to 3+
resident workgroups nothing fills that stall. Two bit-identical arms
follow (KERNELS.md G2): strength-reduce the addresses (every fragment
advances by a constant per iteration; 85 instructions become 12 adds),
and prefetch one K tile ahead into a second register set (+24 VGPRs,
which at WN 8 forces a 4×4 or 8×4 grid). Whether the remaining ~10
points are latency (the prefetch takes them too) or L2/MALL bandwidth
(only traffic per FLOP does) is G-o5.

**Reproduce.** Builds: the shipped `-D` line in `shaders/shaders.go`
plus `-DLDS_PAD=4096|8192|16384` or `-DL0_LOADS=1`, compiled to the
scratchpad (never shipped); `H3_SHAPES=480 H3_PROFILE=1 H3_BANK=q8
H3_GEMM_SPV=path go test ./h3/dit -run TestGPUShapes -v`; the clock
from a 100 ms sampler on `hwmon/freq1_input` and `power1_average` (§0.6).
