## The section number is the address — do not renumber

`§N.M` is a stable identifier, cited **972 times** across this repo: in
`ideas.md` and the archives here, and — the part that matters — in **293 comments
across 29 files** in `shaders/`, `bench/`, `cmd/` and `vk/`, where it is the
only link between a kernel and the experiment that justified its shape. For
example `shaders/gemv_w4a8.comp` cites §1.1, §1.2 and §1.3 to explain three
separate decisions in one file.

So: a finding may be **moved, renamed or rewritten**, but its number is
permanent. A new experiment takes the next free number in its section
rather than reusing or reflowing an old one. Filenames carry the number so
that `grep -rl '§2\.3' .` and `ls research/2.3*` find the same thing.

| [P14](p14-prefill-at-depth.md) | **The gather, and 128k prefills past 900 tok/s — the target met.** A 128 000-cell prefill goes from P13's **563.6** to **826.0 tok/s at ubatch 2048** (1.47x, falloff from depth zero 0.50x → 0.72x) and to **946.1 at 8192**, with decode unmoved as the control. Four changes. **The selection runs over block scores, not cells**: all `ratio` cells of a pooled block carry one score, so a radix select over `nKV/ratio` entries with a per-block *weight* — how many of its cells this token can see, which is closed form — reaches the same threshold, the same tie fill and the same bitmask, and `llm_attn_expand.comp` is then not computed at all. `attn.select` **0.281 → 0.020 ms a token** (13.8x, not the 4x the traffic argument predicts: the histogram is bounded at the last block a cell can reach and the 16 KB LDS cache now holds `ratio` times as many cells), the expansion deleted, and **1.14 GB of arena** freed — bit-identical over four chunk schedules, including the ones that make the incomplete tail and the causal boundary inside a partly visible block both live. **The attention runs over a per-cell gather**: per query tile, the union of its rows' selections compacted ascending with the causal test folded into a per-row mask, then a *dense* attention over the list with no axis walk, no block skip and no causal comparison. **2.15x on `attn.attn`**, which is exactly the cell ratio — 15 145 cells read against a 7 049-cell union, measured with a new `SelUnion` instrument after P13's own estimate of both numbers turned out wrong. It needed **the value plane to become cell-major** first (a selected run is `ratio` consecutive cells, one 128-byte line; transposed it was four halves out of each of sixteen rows), which costs the block kernel 7% and decode nothing, and it stages one head-dim group at a time through 1 KB of LDS because occupancy is the one thing a prefill cannot trade. It is **the first kernel here that is not chunk-invariant and cannot be made so** — its list is the union of sixteen rows, so a chunk ending inside the tile folds the same terms in a different order — so it is pinned off under `PinSchedule` with the other reassociating kernels and gated by an *exact* equality on the set plus a tolerance on the answer (4.1e-06 rms against 9.9e-04 from llama.cpp). **The indexer's score moves to the matrix cores**, a GEMM per head with a rectifier: 0.077 → 0.034, chunk-invariant, and marginally nearer llama.cpp's own selection than the scalar kernel it replaces. **And `maxStorageBufferRange` gets an error instead of a comment**, because it is silent when exceeded: the driver clamps the descriptor rather than failing, so a five-depth sweep at ubatch 4096 raised `-ctx` past the cap, attended over a cache of zeros, and came back **1124 tok/s against a true 886** — 1.28x too fast, with a degenerate indexer selecting a contiguous window. *The failure mode is a benchmark that gets faster.* **Four measured refusals, and together they say what the gathered kernel is bound by**: doubling its matrix work costs **2.7%**, a subgroup staging barrier instead of a workgroup one is 1.00x, staging two head-dim groups a barrier is 1.00x (four is 0.75x), and sharing the staged key and value across two query heads of one kv head — halving both the global gather reads and the LDS writes — is **1.04x** (four heads is 0.75x). Not the matrix cores, not latency, not the gather's traffic: what is left is the LDS round-trip for the fragments and the per-head softmax scaffolding, both linear in the union, which is why the cost is linear in the cells visited. |

| [P13](p13-long-context-prefill.md) | **128k completes, and the attention kernel's key block was twice as wide as it should have been.** A 128 000-cell prefill **runs**, for the first time in this vertical, at **471.7 tok/s** — and the thing stopping it was never `DispatchMultiMarked`'s marks, it was that **P0's submit budget had no depth term in it**. Every measurement behind P0's `322 us a dispatch + 582 ns a dispatch-row` was taken from cell zero, so `batchFor(rows)` went on handing back the same 660 dispatches at 2048 rows whether the cache was empty or held 128 000 cells; at 64 000 those 660 already held the gfx ring for ~1.53 s of the 2 s watchdog while the budget believed it was spending 0.86, and at ~115 000 they cross it. The missing term is **10.46 ns per (row, cell)** — read straight off P12's own ladder, 1.696 s a pass at depth zero against 3.067 at 64 000 — and **only the attention dispatches carry it**, because every other block is flat in depth (P11-5). So the chunker walks the recorded sequence and cuts where the *accumulated* prediction reaches the budget, charging each dispatch what its own label costs, and rescales itself against what the last submit actually measured — **upwards only**, because the affine constant is an order out at one row and a scale that learned from decode steps would hand the next prefill four times its budget. Then **the rung**: L2f chose `qt1_kt2` on 512-token prefills from cell zero, where a 32-cell key block is two cooperative-matrix tiles of reuse against one. At depth that is the wrong ladder — what the kernel can skip is a (query tile, key block) pair, so the cost is the *union* of sixteen adjacent queries' selections at the block's width, and P11-7's own table says 11.7% of the axis at 16 cells against 17.4% at 32. The new `qt1_kt1` build is **1.61x on `attn.attn` at 128 000 cells** (0.9107 → 0.5659 ms a token), 1.60x at 96 000, 1.36x at 64 000 and 1.19x at 32 000, with `attn.select`, `attn.score` and `attn.expand` identical to three decimals as the control — **471.7 → 563.6 tok/s**, falloff 0.42x → 0.50x, two passes agreeing to 0.05-0.2%. That is P11-7's 1.40x collected as a **rung** after P11-7 lost 1.18x trying to collect it as a branch, and it is the rule that file drew, applied: change the loop's granularity, not what happens inside it. It is **prefill's rung and not decode's** — with it on both, decode at 128k went 22.94 → 22.27, because a decode step costs one wave's serial walk (P8) and halving the block doubles the walk. **The refusal is the idea that looked best going in.** `llm_attn_blocks.comp` compacts the live key blocks into an ascending per-query-tile list so the kernel iterates the list instead of the axis — bit-identical, gated as an equality, deleting **83% of the visits** at 128 000 cells for 0.001 ms a token — and it is **1.00x at every depth**, and 1.05x *against* at the narrow rung where there are twice as many dead blocks to skip. The mask read was never the cost: the same sixteen lines serve all twenty-four heads and the MALL holds the tile's whole 128 KB. Third instance of one rule, now the engine's rather than any kernel's: *latency and redundant reads that matter at decode do not matter at prefill, because prefill has occupancy* — 3072 single-wave workgroups against a decode step's 24. It ships behind `LLM_ATTN_BLOCK_LIST=1` because the decode split and a per-cell gather both want it. **And the arithmetic of 900 tok/s at 128k is now closed-form**: the floor of everything flat in depth is 0.803 ms a token, `attn.attn` is 0.566, and `attn.select` + `attn.expand` are 0.284 and are one change; those plus a 4096 ubatch reach ~700, and **only the per-cell gather reaches 900** — priced at 2.8x on `attn.attn` where before P13 it needed 4.9x. |

Correctness is exact where it can be — the bitmask is `topK`'s selection **cell for cell on all 4096 rows** over the device's own scores, no tolerance — and 0.0381% of visible cells against llama.cpp's, **17x** L4a's CPU figure for a reason upstream of this kernel: **the indexer's two BF16 weights are fp16 column ranges of L2f-3's one fused matrix**, so the device cannot take L4a-6's bf16 activation and `indexer_k_raw` sits at 9.319e-04 — L4a-6's own *unmodelled* number — 2093x its modelled one, 24x on the score. It does not propagate: `attn_output-3` at 4096 tokens is **9.872e-04 rms**, inside the 1.14e-03 the same comparison gives at 7 tokens, and the dense control is **2.0x further**. Two traps the selection creates and the dense path cannot reach: `exp2(-inf - -inf)` is a NaN when a key block has nothing selected, and a pad row has no selection at all. And the attention ladder's **top two rungs invert with length** — qt2_kt4 overtakes qt1_kt4 at 4096 tokens — in the dense column too, so it is length and not sparsity; the winner does not move |
